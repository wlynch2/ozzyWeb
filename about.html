Meet Our Founder

Osvaldo Romero Martinez

Growing up in Villa Rica, I was surrounded by hard work and the construction industry from an early age. My father owns a construction company, and watching him build his business taught me the value of dedication, craftsmanship, and treating people with respect. As a Hispanic entrepreneur, I’m proud of where I come from and the experiences that have shaped me. I also grew up playing sports, which taught me the importance of discipline, teamwork, and never giving up when things get difficult. While my father’s business inspired me to pursue construction, my goal is to create my own path and build something of my own. Through this company, I’m committed to providing quality drywall services, earning the trust of every customer, and creating a business that I can be proud of for years to come.
